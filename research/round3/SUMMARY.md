## Meta-lesson after 3 rounds (12 theses killed)
1. AI-infra / agent-tooling ideas visible through desk research are already funded by Oct 2026, often 5+ startups plus a platform feature.
2. Surviving gaps sit in (a) industries VCs underweight where AI can do the work, (b) new cost shocks (e.g., electricity capacity prices up ~10x in PJM since 2024 due to data centers), (c) second-order effects of products in hypergrowth *right now*.
3. Round 4 changes method: start from published VC wishlists (VC clarity by construction) and from underweighted industries, then check crowding.
