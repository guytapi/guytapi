# Discovery Kit: Counter-Signed Records for Agent-to-Agent Deals (Outcome B)

Goal of the 14 days: find out whether suppliers already have agent-made commitments, whether the record gap costs real money, and whether anyone will pay. If not, kill the thesis and the whole agent-commerce family.

## 1. Target list (build 40 names, contact 25, aim for 15 calls)

**Who:** suppliers with $200M–$5B revenue selling to enterprises that run agentic sourcing (Keelvar, Pactum, Fairmarkit, Coupa Navi, SAP Ariba Joule, Zip).

| Segment | Example company types | Role to reach |
|---|---|---|
| Freight (brokers, asset carriers, 3PLs) | Mid-size truckload brokers, LTL carriers, ocean NVOCCs | CFO, VP Pricing, GC |
| Packaging | Corrugated, flexible packaging, labels | CFO, VP Sales Ops, GC |
| MRO / industrial distribution | Regional MRO distributors, fastener/electrical distributors | CFO, VP eCommerce, GC |

**How to find them:**
- Keelvar, Pactum and Fairmarkit case studies and webinars name buyers; their suppliers are listed in buyer supplier-diversity pages and award announcements.
- LinkedIn: "bid automation", "RFQ automation", "pricing agent", "quote agent" in supplier job posts (signals they already run a bid agent).
- Freight: lanes on Keelvar/Transfix tenders; DAT and FreightWaves coverage of AI tendering.

Plus: 5 audit partners (Big-4 and mid-tier) who cover distributors or logistics; 1 call each with Keelvar and Pactum partnerships teams.

## 2. Outreach

**CFO / Controller email**
> Subject: Your bid agents are signing deals without a signature
>
> Several of your customers now run AI sourcing agents, and if your team uses a bid or quote agent, deals are closing in hours with no human signature. The only record of what your company agreed to sits on your customer's platform. We're studying how suppliers prove authority, terms and delivery for agent-made commitments. Could I get 25 minutes to compare notes? Happy to share what we're seeing across freight, packaging and MRO.

**General Counsel email**
> Subject: Who authorized that award?
>
> When your bid agent and a customer's sourcing agent close a deal, which document shows your agent had authority to bind the company, and which terms applied? We're interviewing GCs at suppliers about this gap and the new AAA Legal Context Protocol. 25 minutes?

**Audit partner email**
> Subject: Auditing agent-made sales commitments
>
> As suppliers' bid agents start committing revenue without human signatures, how would you test authority and completeness for those commitments? Is the customer platform's log enough evidence? 20 minutes of your view would help our research.

## 3. Interview script (suppliers, 25 minutes)

Never ask "would you use this?". Ask for facts, data and next steps.

1. "How many commitments did your bid or quote agents make last quarter, through which customer platforms, and for what total value?" (Ask for a number; then: "Could you export that list today?")
2. "Walk me through the last disputed award (price, lane, quantity, terms). Whose record settled it? What did it cost?"
3. "Does your delegation-of-authority policy mention agents? Has your auditor or GC asked about it?"
4. "Would you accept your largest customer's platform log as the only record of what you agreed to? What do you do today instead?"
5. "If a customer asked you to prove your agent's authority to bind you, what would you send?"
6. "Do your agents also accept vendor terms or usage commitments when signing up for services? Any over $10K?" (B+ module check.)
7. Close: "Could we run a back-test on 90 days of your agent commitments under NDA? If we find exceptions worth more than 1% of value, would you pilot at $15K for 60 days?"

## 4. Back-test spec (for any supplier that shares data)

**Inputs:** 90 days of agent commitment events (bid submissions, awards), award notices from buyer platforms, ERP orders, shipment/delivery records, invoices and credit memos, delegation-of-authority policy.

**Checks:**
| Check | Exception if |
|---|---|
| Authority | Commitment value or terms exceed the agent's delegated limits, or no limit exists |
| Terms | Award terms differ from the terms the supplier believes applied (price, quantity, lane, payment terms) |
| Reconciliation | Award vs ERP order vs delivery vs invoice mismatch |
| Evidence | No supplier-side, tamper-evident record exists for the award |
| Disputes | Credit memos or disputes linked to agent-made awards |

**Output:** exception value as % of committed value, dispute dollars, and an example evidence pack.

## 5. Scorecard (decide on day 14)

| Criterion | Pass threshold | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Suppliers confirming agent-made commitments | ≥5 of 15 | |
| Suppliers sharing data | ≥2 | |
| Exceptions found | ≥1% of committed value or ≥$50K disputes | |
| Paid pilots or LOIs | ≥2 at ≥$15K | |
| Buyer willing to counter-sign | ≥1 | |
| Auditor says platform log insufficient | ≥1 | |

**Kill if any:** fewer than 5 of 15 confirm agent commitments; exceptions <0.5% with no disputes; Keelvar/Pactum confirm an exportable two-party ledger auditors accept; DocuSign announces agent-commitment sealing for sourcing; counsel finds unavoidable AB 325 exposure.

**If it passes:** re-score all 11 categories with the real evidence. Advancing to A requires Pain, ROI clarity and Urgency ≥9 from the data, not from desk research.
