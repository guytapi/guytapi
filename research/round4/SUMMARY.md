## What the pattern says
1. In Oct 2026, any AI-tooling gap visible in public sources is funded within ~6 months, and platforms (GitHub, Cursor, Anthropic, OpenAI, AWS, Microsoft, Salesforce, ServiceNow, HackerOne, Ramp, Stripe, Cloudflare) ship the obvious control layer.
2. Ideas that score best on "pain" and "why now" score worst on "competition" and "defensibility": the obvious pain attracts everyone.
3. Desk research systematically finds the obvious. The remaining edge has to come from (a) proprietary customer access/insight, or (b) industries where VCs are underweight but an AI-native operator has already proven the model elsewhere (Avoca, EliseAI, HappyRobot pattern).
