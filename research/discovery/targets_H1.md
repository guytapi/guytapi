# H1 Outreach Targets: Supervision at AI App Companies

Built 2026-10-06 for the H1 outreach spec in `DISCOVERY_MACHINE.md` (Part 5). Company level only: no personal names or contact details were collected.

**How this was built:** 35 web searches covering funding news, job boards (startup.jobs, voiceaispace, fastaijobs, YC jobs, builtin) and YC directory snippets. Pages were not fetched, so every fact below comes from a search-result snippet. The source URL is the one the snippet came from.

**How to read it:**
- **Fit A** = matches the spec on stage, vertical and signal.
- **Fit B** = matches, but one dimension is off (stage too early or late, too many customers, unclear size, or an infra/platform model). Still worth contacting, but qualify on the first call.
- **Website "(unverified)"** = the domain is a best guess and did not appear in a search result. Check it before outreach.
- **Size:** only a few companies had headcount in the snippets. "~" values are estimates from stage and round size. Check them on LinkedIn.
- **Job-post dates:** postings were seen in search results, but their dates could not be confirmed. Re-check that each one is within 90 days before using it as an opener.
- **Target roles (all rows):** CEO, CTO, VP Operations, Head of Deployments / Customer Engineering. Rows that add a role name it.

## Where to find more
1. **fastaijobs.com stage filters.** The Series A FDE filter showed about 135 roles at 76 companies, and Series B about 160 roles at 69 companies (Aug 2026): https://www.fastaijobs.com/jobs/stage/series-a/forward-deployed-engineer and https://www.fastaijobs.com/jobs/stage/series-b/forward-deployed-engineer. Its operations-role filter is useful for "AI ops / agent ops" titles.
2. **voiceaispace.com/jobs/forward-deployed** for voice-agent companies hiring FDEs.
3. **YC jobs board.** Search "forward deployed" and filter to the 2023–2026 batches.
4. **Funding digests:** neuronfeed.com/category/ai-customer-support, Axios Pro fintech deals (finance-ops agents), and the qubit.capital weekly Series B+ roundups.
5. **LinkedIn job search** for "deployment strategist", "agent operations" and "AI operations" with "AI agent". Search snippets could not surface these at startups; only Salesforce came up for "deployment strategist".
6. **Before outreach:** verify the websites marked "(unverified)", confirm job-post dates are within 90 days, and confirm headcount is 30–400 on LinkedIn.
