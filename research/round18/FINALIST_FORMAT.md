# Finalist format (founder-defined)

For every finalist provide:
- one-line problem
- why this problem exists now
- exact buyer
- exact ICP
- current workaround
- why incumbents cannot easily own it
- 30-day MVP
- pilot design
- pricing hypothesis
- expansion path
- moat
- why it could become a $10B+ company
- direct competitors and adjacent threats
- the one sentence to send a CTO (or CFO)
- 5 customer discovery questions
- hard kill criteria

Plus: scores 1-10 on Pain, Urgency, ROI clarity, Customer accessibility, Pilot speed, Market size, Expansion, Venture potential, Defensibility, Why now, Competition position (bar: avg ≥8.5, none <7).

Outcome must be classified as:
A. scores ≥8.5 after deep research, or
B. genuinely new, unresolved, too early for public evidence, with a precise 14-day customer discovery test that could reveal whether it is massive, or
KILL.

Ideal shape: behavior that barely existed 12 months ago + rapid adoption + new unavoidable bottleneck + no obvious category owner + fast integration + buyer with budget + compounding data/network/workflow moat.
