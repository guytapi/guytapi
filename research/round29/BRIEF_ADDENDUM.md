# Round 29 addendum (read after round28/BRIEF.md)

Each agent plays THREE roles in sequence and must record all three views in its file:
1. First-principles product thinker: push the hypothesis to 2028-2029 (hundreds/thousands of agents), map 2nd/3rd-order consequences, propose 2-3 candidate infrastructure layers.
2. Technical infrastructure architect: where does the system break at 100x; what primitive is missing (cf. Kafka, Stripe, OpenTelemetry moments).
3. Skeptical VC partner: where is the money, who captures value, what would make me lead the seed, what stops me.
Then self-red-team the best thesis against competitors (search hard, Jul-Oct 2026) and write it in finalist format with honest scores and A/B/KILL.

Lessons from round 28 red team (do not repeat):
- Coordination tooling for agent fleets formed this quarter (Korso Shepherd, Tether, Coordinaut, Augment Cosmos).
- Change tracking/rollback is owned by Datadog Bits Release, Harness, Liquibase, Neon checkpoints.
- "Neutral record for third parties" theses fail because the money goes to the licensed signer (auditor, insurer, AIUC), not the record keeper.
- Anything a platform team can build in a sprint is dead.
