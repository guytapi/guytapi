# Round 31 brief: replacement theses (founder-defined)

Goal: find massive EXISTING software/infrastructure categories ($1B-$100B) that must be rebuilt because AI agents invalidate a core assumption. Competition is acceptable. Kill only if: incumbent can add it trivially; switching has no advantage; urgency weak; wedge needs years of enterprise adoption; market can't become enormous.

Assumptions that may break: humans are the users / initiate actions / have identities / review important work / create most software; workflows change slowly; software output is scarce; API calls come from deterministic apps; more humans than agents; employees use SaaS through GUIs; humans move info between systems; changes at human speed; companies buy software per employee; customers discover products themselves; humans accountable for every decision.

Markets: cloud, DevOps, dev infra, security, identity, IT ops, data infra, observability, testing, APIs, enterprise SaaS, support, sales infra, product mgmt, collaboration, knowledge mgmt, commerce infra, procurement, marketplaces, search/discovery, software distribution, non-regulated payments infra, AI infra. Avoid banking/insurance/gov/defense as initial markets.

Negative training data: research/STATUS.md (≈115 killed theses). Don't repeat a killed thesis unless architecture/business model changes fundamentally.

Each thesis must include: Existing category; Market size; Old assumption; Why AI breaks it; New category; Product (CTO-simple); Buyer title; Pain; Recent evidence (≥5 signals, last 90 days if possible); Current workaround; Competitors (direct/adjacent); Why incumbents may lose (structural); Wedge; Integration (hours/days?); 30-day pilot; Pricing; Expansion; Moat at 10/100/1,000 customers; $10B case; CTO one-liner; Scores 1-10: Market size, Market transformation, Urgency, Why-now, Ease of reaching buyer, Speed to pilot, Ease of integration, Competitive opening, Structural differentiation, Expansion potential, Moat, VC attractiveness (+ average).

Shape of a winner: "A $10B-$100B category currently works like X because the world assumed Y. AI makes Y false. Therefore the category must be rebuilt as Z."
