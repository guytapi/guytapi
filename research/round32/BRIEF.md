# Round 32 brief: company-level reconstruction (MARKET → COMPANY)

Unit of research = one fast-growing AI-native company. For each, reconstruct where engineering/ops headcount and internal tooling go that "should not exist".

Per company (keep to ~12 lines):
1. What changed in last 12 months (growth, product, scale)
2. Teams growing fast / roles hired repeatedly (cite job posts if found)
3. Internal platforms publicly described (eng blogs, talks)
4. Human ops work still required; spreadsheets/Slack/queues/scripts/manual approvals
5. What architecture must do that normal SaaS never needed
6. What scales linearly with customers / agent executions / generated code / tool calls / integrations / deployments / exceptions / enterprise requirements
7. Complaints (HN, GitHub issues, blogs, podcasts, docs)
8. Capability built internally a normal company couldn't afford
9. Pain tags (short kebab tags, reused across companies so clusters emerge)

Per discovered pain, when possible: PEOPLE COST, INFRA COST, FREQUENCY, SCALING LAW, CURRENT SOLUTION.

Rules: establish workflow + economic pain first; do NOT search for startups solving it. Competition allowed. No banks/insurance/government/defense.

Cluster filter: ≥5 unrelated companies, ≥2 built internally, grows with AI adoption, removes headcount/infra/risk, buyer today, deploy in 30 days, measurable ROI, large category.

Output: 15 hidden pain clusters → 5 finalists with a money/headcount email claim → ranking on 10 criteria.
